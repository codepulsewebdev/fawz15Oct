About Us

The FAWZ™ brand was established in 1981 as a family business. From very humble beginnings and facing the usual challenges experienced by many family businesses, by the grace of the Almighty, the company has grown to become one of the leading suppliers of haberdashery to the Southern African market.

Product Range

From our initial range, we have grown to offer an extensive selection of haberdashery products. We continue striving to meet market demands by expanding our range of items. Our products include needles, elastics, threads, interlining, machine accessories, sewing notions and much more.

Sourcing

All our products are imported from China. Through many years of importing, we have built trusted relationships with our suppliers. Each supplier is committed to providing quality products within highly competitive pricing structures. We have also established an office in China to support better sourcing and quality control.

Quality

Our quality standards are carefully balanced with affordable pricing. As the global market becomes more competitive, consumers continue to demand reasonably priced and dependable products. We also offer a selection of premium-quality products and maintain firm agreements with our suppliers regarding quality and quantity.

Our Focus

Our focus is to establish FAWZ as a trusted haberdashery brand offering reasonable quality at competitive prices. Our target market is the Southern African region, and our customers include manufacturers, wholesalers and retailers. The market response has been excellent and continues to guide our way forward. We remain committed to offering the best possible service.
